# Instructions for every research session

Read `authoritative/START_HERE.md` before doing work. Committed repository
records, rather than conversation history or model memory, carry research
authority. A pasted prompt is an entry point, not a substitute for reading them.
New explicit instructions from John govern the task; record changes to scope or
preferences before relying on them in subsequent sessions.

## Non-negotiable scope

- Goal: a distinct, worthwhile mathematical contribution that reaches genuine
  journal peer review, acceptance and publication. Acceptance is externally
  controlled and must never be promised or inferred from a preprint.
- Initial territory: inverse zero-sum problems in combinatorial number theory.
  The current exact target is carried by `authoritative/STATE.json` and the
  target register; do not infer it from initialization text or conversation
  history. Existing projects supply workflow lessons, not an automatic
  mathematical starting point.
- Before mathematical computation or proof development, establish the target
  by name, genre, type and exact scope; an eligible, relevant publication
  shortlist; and explicit willingness for external proposal and manuscript
  review. The initial comparison must not rank targets by complexity or
  probability of obtaining a proof.
- The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics is the leading journal candidate,
  not a submission decision or an acceptance prediction. Exclude journals
  prohibiting substantive AI contributions; copy-editing-only permission is
  insufficient. Unclear policies are pending, not confirmed eligibility.
- The external mathematician's intended role is independent pre-submission
  reviewer. Journal referees are selected by editors. Record and disclose prior
  involvement as required; never promise that our reviewer will referee for the
  journal. Authorship must follow actual contributions if the role changes.

## Autonomous work and closeout

Follow `docs/SESSION_PROTOCOL.md` and `docs/CLOSEOUT_TEMPLATE.md`.
Do the bounded authorized work, validate it, and checkpoint useful results
without waiting for John to say "finish up". Do not ask approval for routine,
reversible research documentation or checkpointing already within scope.
Do not start another numbered session in the same turn.

**If any active blocker requires John's input or action, report it explicitly,
ask him to fulfil the concrete requirement, and output NO next-session prompt.**
This prohibits research, resolution-only, conditional, and placeholder kickoff
prompts while such a blocker is active. The presence of a prompt means it is
ready to copy and run immediately. Never silently assume an unanswered decision,
a sent message, a reply, reviewer willingness, or author approval.

Contacting people is not authorized by a research kickoff alone. Prepare a
reviewable outreach draft and recipient verification when useful, then ask John
to send it or explicitly instruct sending. No emails, messages, invitations,
submissions, or public endorsements on his behalf without applicable explicit
authorization. Willingness to email is not an instruction to send an email.

Before every session: pin the live authority commit, reconcile any mismatch
with the pasted prompt, read the required records, and confirm the session is
unique. Before every closeout: synchronize state, claims, sources, decisions,
blockers and the session ledger; check repository integrity; commit and verify
the remote checkpoint. Preserve failures and specify the next useful task.

## Research integrity

Primary literature is required for theorem scope and novelty comparisons.
Separate abstract-only access, full statement inspection and proof inspection.
Never infer that a problem is open from unsuccessful searching. Preserve exact
quantifiers, model definitions, hypotheses, exceptional cases and dependencies.
Distinguish source results, programme proofs, conjectures, conditional results,
experimental evidence, formal verification and human review.
Formalisation verifies the encoded statement under its trust boundary; it does
not establish novelty, significance, semantic correspondence or journal review.
Never treat repeated LLM agreement as independent mathematical peer review.

No mathematical experiments, searches over examples, new proof attempts or Lean
development during preflight. Document validation and ordinary file operations
are permitted. Recheck sources and policies when a decision depends on current
status. Preserve a transparent account of substantive AI use.

Use the existing Apache-2.0 licence. Do not publish private correspondence,
addresses or promises; preserve only the minimum necessary contact-status
summary unless John explicitly authorizes more. Repository rename and target
change must be logged and internal references updated.
