## Primary / controlling sources

1. B. Girard and W. A. Schmid, *Direct zero-sum problems for certain groups of rank three*, J. Number Theory 197 (2019), 297--316, DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2018.08.016, arXiv:1806.07636.
   - Full relevant statement/proof inspected.
   - Establishes conventions, `eta(G_m)=6m+2`, the equal-factor subgroup/quotient upper-bound route, and recalls the unconditional rank-two inverse-`eta` theorem.
   - Does not state the CAND-05 equality-case classification.

2. B. Girard and W. A. Schmid, *Inverse zero-sum problems for certain groups of rank three*, Acta Math. Hungar. 160 (2020), 229--247, DOI 10.1007/s10474-019-00983-w, arXiv:1809.03178.
   - Full relevant theorem/proof inspected.
   - Solves inverse `eta` for `C_2+C_2+C_{2n}`, not CAND-05.
   - Records the unique `C_2^3` eta endpoint and a cyclic-kernel inductive architecture.

3. W. A. Schmid, *Restricted inverse zero-sum problems in groups of rank 2*, Q. J. Math. 63 (2012), 477--487, DOI 10.1093/qmath/haq042.
   - Historical rank-two inverse source. The exact unconditional form needed here is taken from the later GS2019 theorem statement.
