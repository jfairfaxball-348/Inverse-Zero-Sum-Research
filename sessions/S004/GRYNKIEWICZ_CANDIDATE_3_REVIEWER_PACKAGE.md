## Proposal snapshot

The proposed question is:

Classify every sequence S over C_3^3 minus {0} of length 24 that has no two
innerly non-zero-sum-joint short zero-sum subsequences, where short means
length at most 3.

Source baseline:

1. Gao--Hui--X. Li--Y. Li--Qu--Zhong, Acta Arith. 212 (2024),
   DOI 10.4064/aa230118-1-10, define eta^N and prove
   eta^N(C_3^3)=25. The paper also identifies eta^N with the older
   Narkiewicz-sense eta* invariant.
2. Fan--Hui--Zhong, Colloq. Math. 176 (2024), 207--217,
   DOI 10.4064/cm9413-10-2024, already solves the exact eta^N inverse
   condition for rank-two C_n+C_{nm} with n in {2,3}, m>=2.
3. Fan--Zhong, JCTA 212 (2025), 105984, develops the equivalent innerly-joint
   short-minimal-zero-sum formulation and rank-two theory.
4. Hui--Zhong, JCTA 224 (2026), 106238, completely settles the inverse
   eta^N problem for rank-two finite abelian groups.
5. The S004 bounded search found no exact C_3^3 inverse completion or
   theorem subsuming it, but also found no source explicitly declaring the
   displayed problem open. The programme therefore labels its current status
   SOURCE-DEFINED / CURRENT STATUS UNKNOWN, not "open".

The intended contribution, if the status survives expert review, is a complete
structural classification (or a comparably decisive structural reduction) of
all length-24 extremals, with multiplicities/support/forms explicit. A
computer-only catalogue or isolated configuration is not the intended result.

The leading publication candidate is the Electronic Journal of Combinatorics;
JCTA is a credible backup. This is subject/workflow fit only, not an
acceptance prediction.

The project uses substantive AI assistance for literature analysis and, only
after preflight gates open, may use it in mathematical investigation. Source
claims and any future proofs must be independently checked by the human author,
and AI use will be disclosed according to the selected journal's current
