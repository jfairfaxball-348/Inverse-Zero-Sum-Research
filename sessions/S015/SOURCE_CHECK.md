# S015 focused source check

Date: 2026-10-04.

This check was triggered because S015-P1 is an exchange/replacement theorem and
could conceivably be a standard inverse-EGZ lemma. It is deliberately narrow;
S013 remains the latest broad route/current-status comparison.

## Rechecked / inspected

1. **Girard--Schmid 2019**, *Direct zero-sum problems for certain groups of
   rank three*, JNT 197 (2019), Theorem 3.2. The exact equal-factor threshold
   `s(G_m)=8m+1` remains the only published theorem used in the ambient
   extension argument, through S014.
2. **Gao--Hong--Peng 2022**, *On zero-sum subsequences of length k exp(G) II*,
   JCTA 187 (2022), 105563. Publisher abstract/introduction and surfaced main
   inverse statements were rechecked as the closest general fixed-length
   inverse literature already indexed as ZS-42. The inspected statements
   concern structural inverse results under their own `s_{k exp(G)}`
   hypotheses; no inspected statement packages the S015 value-class
   saturation/core-transport theorem.
3. Focused current searches used combinations of:
   `replacement core`, `safe replacement`, `one-position replacement`,
   `one-term replacement`, `exchange`, `EGZ extremal`, and
   `inverse zero-sum`. They did not surface an additional primary zero-sum
   theorem statement directly matching S015-P1--P4.

## Evidentiary limit

Item 3 is a search non-hit only. It is **not** evidence that S015-P1--P4 are
novel, that D14-01 is open, that the literature search is complete, or that the
deductions are significant enough for publication. If these lemmas survive
into an actual contribution, they require a result-specific literature and
novelty audit in P4.
