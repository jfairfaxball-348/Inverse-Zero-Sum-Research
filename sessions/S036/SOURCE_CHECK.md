## ZS-67 — Schmid 2011

Wolfgang A. Schmid, The Inverse Problem Associated to the Davenport Constant for C_2+C_2+C_{2n}, and Applications to the Arithmetical Characterization of Class Groups, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 18(1) (2011), P33.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.37236/520
Official article: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v18i1p33

The official PDF was inspected at Theorem 3.1 and proof Step 1. The relevant transfer says that if A is minimal zero-sum and A=F_1...F_k lifts a factorisation of its quotient image, then the block-sum sequence product sigma(F_i) is minimal zero-sum over the subgroup kernel. The same source records the complete elementary-2-group atom form and explicitly warns that this kernel class gives little rigidity for this method.

## ZS-61 — Hui--Zhong 2026

Wanzhen Hui and Qinghai Zhong, On the inverse problem of the Narkiewicz-sense eta-constant for finite abelian groups of rank 2, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 224 (2026), 106238.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106238

The current publisher version was checked. Theorem 1.1 treats G=C_n^2 and a nonzero sequence of length 3n-1 with no innerly non-zero-sum-joint short zero-sum subsequences and gives explicit basis normal forms. The common-torsion quotient already contains the forbidden joint-short configuration, so the inverse hypothesis fails.

## ZS-57 — Girard--Schmid 2020

Benjamin Girard and Wolfgang A. Schmid, Inverse zero-sum problems for certain groups of rank three, Acta Mathematica Hungarica 160 (2020), 229--247.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10474-019-00983-w
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03178

Theorem 4.1 was rechecked. For C_2+C_2+C_{2n}, it classifies exactly the length eta(G)-1=2n+3 sequences with no short zero-sum subsequence. A quotient of G_m by an order-m subgroup can have this isomorphism type, but original short-zero-sum avoidance does not imply quotient avoidance because quotient-zero subsequences may lift to nonzero kernel sums.
