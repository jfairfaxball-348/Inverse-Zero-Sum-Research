### S014–S016: new ambient replacement-core theory

S014 is the strongest all-m structural advance in this audit period. From the
exact EGZ threshold it proves full (n-1)-sum coverage, support-copy
essentiality, the exact replacement-core criterion
`S_{p->x}` extremal iff `p in K_x(S)`, and forbids multiplicity n-2.

S015 proves that every replacement core is a union of complete value classes,
safe replacement transports the entire representation family and core exactly,
nontrivial support incidence forces stronger multiplicity bounds and exact

S016 packages a hypothetical near-full nontrivial core into one threshold

This chain is materially different from the stopped kernel/capacity route and

### S017–S019: 2-primary algebraic counting and its ceiling

S017 source-checks the Gao–Geroldinger p-group recurrence and correctly
restricts its direct use to n=2m a power of two. It yields modulo-4 threshold
and deletion-star relations and modulo-8 all-length incidence divisibility.
Sylow projection is explicitly rejected as a full-group mixed-primary transfer.

S018 separates singleton 2n/3n parities, derives the n-wise combined
four-layer binary incidence law, and records the exact source ceiling:
kappa=2 fails after two deletions at equality in the strict length bound and
kappa=3 is unavailable. The residual S016 reservoir/graph is recovered only as
a combined binary shadow.

S019 then tests that shadow in the correct abstract design space. The exact
Wilson–Frankl mod-2 ranks and explicit n-to-3n / 4n-to-2n / odd-null lifts
show that every S016 delta=0,1,2 binary signature is realizable by abstract
2n/3n block families. This is a design-realization no-go, not a zero-sum
realization. It does not settle the modulo-4 split, exclude a deficit,
construct a CAND extremal, or transfer to mixed-primary n.
