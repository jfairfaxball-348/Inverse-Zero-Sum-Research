## 1. Strategic test imported from S006--S020

S021 does not rank targets merely by apparent proof difficulty. The CAND-02
history shows that a sharp direct theorem and a natural inverse question can
still produce a long sequence of mathematically correct but publication-incomplete
architectures. The reassessment therefore asks whether a target has:

- an exact theorem identity and settled direct/background baseline;
- a credible current-status boundary without treating search silence as openness;
- identifiable machinery that actually touches the target rather than only an
  adjacent invariant;
- room for a contribution-level structural partial result if the full theorem
  resists, without silently changing the target;
- plausible E-JC or comparable significance and venue fit;
- a credible independent status/review route; and
- a lower risk of repeating S006--S020's pattern of accumulating internal lemmas
  without reaching a theorem package that can survive novelty/significance audit.

## 2. CAND-01 refresh — rank-two Property D

### Exact target

For every integer \`n>=2\`, prove Property D for \`C_n^2\`: every sequence of
length \`s(C_n^2)-1=4n-4\` with no \`n\`-term zero sum is \`T^(n-1)\` with
\`|T|=4\`.

### Current source boundary

Gao's rank-two conjecture and the multiplicativity reduction remain the
controlling classical baseline recorded in S002. Jan-Christoph
Schlage-Puchta's 2025 preprint still states the major partial theorem that all
sufficiently large primes have Property D. The bounded S021 refresh through
2026-10-05 found no primary/current source claiming an all-prime completion,
an effective numerical cutoff, or a complete cleanup of the remaining prime
layer. This non-hit is not evidence that the residue is open or novel.

The strategic issue from S002 is unchanged: the full named conjecture has
strong provenance and high significance if completed, but the recent theorem
compresses any remaining obstruction into a finite, unspecified exceptional
prime layer below a non-effective cutoff. An isolated prime case is not a
reliable contribution target unless it introduces reusable structure or closes
a demonstrably complete residual set.

### S021 assessment

- **Baseline confidence:** high.
- **Current-status confidence:** medium; the conjectural identity is classical,
  but the exact remaining finite layer is not source-enumerated.
- **Novelty risk:** medium, especially for finite-prime cleanup or any claimed
  effective threshold.
- **Entry machinery:** strong rank-two inverse machinery and multiplicativity,
  but the 2025 large-prime theorem itself does not expose a finite checklist.
- **Meaningful partial-result scope:** credible only if structural/effective and
  reusable; weak for isolated computational cases.
- **Publication significance:** very high for a genuine all-prime completion;
  potentially much lower for a narrow exceptional case.
- **Reviewer route:** Pingzhi Yuan or David J. Grynkiewicz are cleaner
  independent first-status leads than treating Schlage-Puchta, the closest
  progress author, as automatically independent.
- **Programme risk:** medium-high. The target is central, but the residual
  geometry is less operationally explicit than the full statement suggests.

**Disposition:** viable alternative; not recommended first.

## 3. CAND-03 refresh — inverse Narkiewicz-sense eta on \`C_3^3\`

### Exact target

Classify all sequences \`S\` over \`C_3^3\\{0}\` with
\`|S|=eta^N(C_3^3)-1=24\` that contain no two innerly non-zero-sum-joint short
zero-sum subsequences, where short means length at most \`exp(C_3^3)=3\`.
The desired output is a necessity-and-sufficiency structural theorem with all
multiplicity/support forms explicit. Automorphisms may be used where justified;
translation is not silently imposed. A computational orbit catalogue by itself
is not the target.

### Current source boundary

Gao--Hui--Li--Li--Qu--Zhong (2024) supplies the generalized Narkiewicz
framework and the direct value \`eta^N(C_3^3)=25\`, as audited in S004. The
nearby theory has since become especially clean: Fan--Zhong (2025) states that
larger-rank values have only a few results and develops the rank-two theory,
and Hui--Zhong (JCTA 224, 2026, 106238) completely settles the inverse
Narkiewicz-sense \`eta\` problem for all finite abelian groups of rank two.

Focused S021 searches through 2026-10-05 using \`eta^N\`, \`eta*\`, revised and
Narkiewicz-sense terminology, innerly joint/minimal formulations, \`C_3^3\`,
rank three and higher rank found no checked source that classifies the exact
length-24 \`C_3^3\` extremals or a broader theorem that visibly subsumes them.
Current publication lists in the controlling source line likewise surfaced the
2026 rank-two completion, not a rank-three completion. None of these non-hits
is an openness or novelty certificate, and S021 still did not locate an
explicit primary source declaring this exact case open.

### S021 assessment

- **Baseline confidence:** high: exact direct threshold and exact forbidden
  configuration are settled and published.
- **Current-status confidence:** medium: the source line is current and focused,
  but exact openness is not explicitly certified.
- **Novelty risk:** medium; alternate \`eta*\`/factorization terminology remains
  a real search hazard and must stay in any result-level literature audit.
- **Entry machinery:** unusually concrete. The exponent is three, the forbidden
  configurations have lengths two and three, the rank-two inverse theory is
  complete, and classical \`C_3^3\` zero-sum structure is available as background.
  No transfer from those results is assumed without proof.
- **Meaningful partial-result scope:** stronger than for the other survivors.
  A theorem forcing every extremal into a small structural family, with
  necessity conditions that materially reduce the rank-three inverse problem,
  could be contribution-level if a fresh actual-result audit confirms novelty;
  a mere finite catalogue would not be enough.
- **Publication significance/fit:** strong. This is a first-natural rank-three
  extension immediately beyond a completed rank-two inverse theory, with an
  additional factorization-theoretic identity. E-JC remains a plausible leading
  venue and JCTA is a natural comparable venue because it published the nearest
  rank-two papers.
- **Reviewer route:** David J. Grynkiewicz remains the strongest independent
  first status/proposal lead from the existing audit, with Pingzhi Yuan a strong
  alternative. Hui, Zhong and the defining-paper authors are exact status
  experts but their proximity to the baseline requires an independence/conflict
  assessment before any reviewer role.
- **Programme risk:** medium and materially lower than CAND-02. The group and
  threshold are fixed and exact, so the programme can impose early structural
  milestones and stop conditions rather than launch another all-parameter
  architecture by default.

**Disposition:** strongest survivor and S021 recommendation.

## 5. New candidate admitted by the bounded discovery pass — CAND-05

### Exact target

For every \`m>=2\`, classify all sequences \`S\` over
\`G_m=C_2\\oplus C_{2m}\\oplus C_{2m}\` of length
\`eta(G_m)-1=6m+1\` having no nonempty zero-sum subsequence of length at most
\`exp(G_m)=2m\`.

This is the ordinary inverse \`eta\` problem for the equal-factor rank-three
family, not CAND-02's ordinary inverse EGZ problem.

### Source basis and current boundary

Girard--Schmid's direct rank-three theorem gives
\`eta(C_2\\oplus C_{2m}\\oplus C_{2m})=6m+2\` for every \`m>=1\`; hence the target
has no hidden direct-threshold prerequisite. Their paper recalls mature inverse
\`eta\` structure in rank at most two and notes the special \`m=1\` endpoint, while
their 2020 inverse rank-three paper concerns the non-isomorphic family
\`C_2\\oplus C_2\\oplus C_{2n}\`.

S013 already found no checked equal-factor ordinary-\`eta\` equality/core theorem
that solved the relevant structural problem. S021 repeated focused searches on
the exact group, \`eta(G)-1\`, \`6m+1\`, inverse \`eta\`, short zero sums and recent
rank-three work and found no checked full classification. This is again only a
bounded source non-hit; S021 did not locate an explicit source certifying the
all-\`m\` inverse problem open.

### Assessment

- **Baseline confidence:** high; direct \`eta\` is exact and unconditional.
- **Current-status confidence:** low-medium; source-defined extension, not an
  explicit open question in the checked material.
- **Novelty risk:** medium-high because nearby rank-three and \`disc\` literature
  uses the same ambient groups under different forbidden conditions.
- **Entry machinery:** better aligned than CAND-02 in one important sense: the
  extremal sequence avoids *all* zero sums through the exponent, a stronger
  structural hypothesis than exact-length EGZ avoidance. The direct proof and
  rank-two inverse-\`eta\` machinery provide identifiable starting points, but no
  transfer theorem is assumed.
- **Meaningful partial-result scope:** an infinite arithmetic subfamily or a
  structural reduction valid for all \`m\` could be significant; isolated small
  \`m\` catalogues would not meet the programme bar.
- **Publication fit:** plausible E-JC/JNT subject fit if a substantial theorem
  is obtained.
- **Reviewer route:** Girard and Schmid are exact baseline experts but are close
  to the source problem; Grynkiewicz is a plausible independent first-status
  lead after a fresh conflict check.
- **Programme risk:** high. The target is still an all-\`m\` rank-three
  classification on the same ambient family that consumed S006--S020, so it
  could reproduce a long multi-architecture programme despite the stronger
  short-zero-sum hypothesis.

**Disposition:** viable new alternative, but below CAND-03 and CAND-01.

## 8. Shortlist and recommendation

The strongest shortlist is:

1. **CAND-03 — recommended:** inverse Narkiewicz-sense \`eta\` on \`C_3^3\`, exact
   length 24 structural classification.
2. **CAND-01 — viable alternative:** full all-modulus rank-two Property D,
   understood operationally as eliminating the remaining prime layer by a
   reusable structural/effective theorem rather than accumulating isolated cases.
3. **CAND-05 — viable alternative:** ordinary inverse \`eta\` on
   \`C_2\\oplus C_{2m}\\oplus C_{2m}\`, \`m>=2\`.

### Why CAND-03 is recommended

CAND-03 best balances theorem identity, settled background, current source
visibility, contribution-level structure and programme risk. It is not merely
"easier": its advantage is that the direct invariant and rank-two inverse
programme are complete, the next target is an exact fixed rank-three object,
and a useful structural theorem can be defined without silently solving a
second direct problem or depending on an unspecified exceptional set. That
creates clearer early stop/promote tests than CAND-02 did.

The main residual risk is novelty/status rather than a hidden theorem
prerequisite: S021 still lacks an explicit primary statement declaring the
exact \`C_3^3\` case open. If the owner selects it, all later proof work must
preserve that uncertainty, keep \`eta*\`/factorization aliases in literature
checks, and pause if external or source feedback reveals overlap.

CAND-01 remains a serious alternative because a full completion would be
classically significant and has stronger named-conjecture provenance. It is
not first because the 2025 large-prime theorem leaves the operational residual
layer non-explicit and makes many plausible partial results too narrow for the
programme's publication objective.

CAND-05 is retained because its direct threshold is clean and the short-zero-
sum-free hypothesis gives better structural leverage than CAND-02. It is not
first because it remains an all-parameter rank-three family on the same ambient
groups, with weaker current-status certification and a substantial risk of
another long architecture.
