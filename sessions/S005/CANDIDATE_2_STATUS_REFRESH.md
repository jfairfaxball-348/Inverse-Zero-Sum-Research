## 1. Exact object rechecked

For m >= 2 let

G_m = C_2 + C_{2m} + C_{2m}.

The ordinary Erdős--Ginzburg--Ziv exponent is exp(G_m)=2m. The selected inverse
problem asks for a structural classification of every sequence S over G_m with

|S| = 8m

and no zero-sum subsequence of length 2m.

The direct threshold remains the Girard--Schmid value s(G_m)=8m+1. Thus the
target is the extremal s(G)-1 layer. The programme seeks an all-m structural
classification or a broad uniform theorem materially resolving it, not a
routine isolated small-m catalogue.

## 2. Controlling ordinary-EGZ baseline

The S003 statement-level audit remains controlling.

- Benjamin Girard and Wolfgang A. Schmid, *Direct zero-sum problems for
  certain groups of rank three*, J. Number Theory 197 (2019), 297--316,
  DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2018.08.016, covers
  C_2 + C_{n_2} + C_{n_3} with 2 | n_2 | n_3 and determines s(G) when
  n_2=n_3. The equal-factor specialization gives s(G_m)=8m+1.
- Benjamin Girard and Wolfgang A. Schmid, *Inverse zero-sum problems for
  certain groups of rank three*, Acta Math. Hung. 160 (2020), 229--247,
  DOI 10.1007/s10474-019-00983-w, solves the inverse s(G) and eta(G)
  problems for the different family C_2 + C_2 + C_{2n}. For m>=2 this is
  not the equal-factor family C_2 + C_{2m} + C_{2m}.

The m=1 endpoint is C_2^3 and remains excluded by the selected m>=2 scope.

## 3. 2024--2026 freshness checks

### Li--Yin: same architecture, different invariant

Xue Li and Qiuyu Yin, *On the existence of zero-sum subsequences of distinct
lengths over certain groups of rank three*, Acta Math. Hung. 174 (2024),
323--340, DOI 10.1007/s10474-024-01482-3, studies direct and inverse questions
for disc(G). Its rank-three class C_2 + C_{n_1} + C_{n_2},
2 | n_1 | n_2, contains the CAND-02 equal-factor groups.

This is highly relevant subject/terminology evidence but does not classify
ordinary-EGZ extremals of length s(G)-1. disc(G) asks for zero-sum
subsequences of distinct lengths and must not be substituted for s(G).

### Hui--Li 2026: current inverse activity, rank two and disc(G)

Wanzhen Hui and Xue Li, *The structure of sequences with zero-sum subsequences
of the same length on finite abelian groups of rank two*, Acta Arith. 224
(2026), 221--231, DOI 10.4064/aa251008-9-4, continues the inverse disc(G)
line for C_n + C_{nm}. It is current inverse zero-sum activity and useful
reviewer-fit evidence, but it is rank two and concerns disc(G), not the
ordinary-EGZ target.

### Zhang: recent rank-three prescribed-length direct invariants

Shiwen Zhang, *On some zero-sum invariants for abelian groups of rank three*,
published in 2025 and available as arXiv:2310.05458, studies s_L(G), especially
k-th EGZ and short-zero-sum direct invariants. The inspected abstract,
introduction and theorem descriptions do not present a classification of
ordinary-EGZ extremal sequences for C_2 + C_{2m}^2.

### Girard--Zotova 2025: new direct EGZ work, not inverse structure

Benjamin Girard and Sofia Zotova,
*The Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv constant of rank-two-like p-groups*,
arXiv:2510.23543, gives direct EGZ upper bounds/exact values for
rank-two-like p-groups and certain coprime cyclic products. The inspected
abstract/introduction is a direct-constant result. It does not supply the
structural s(G)-1 inverse classification selected here. For CAND-02 the
equal-factor direct value is already established by Girard--Schmid.

## 5. Theses, conference records and small/subfamily risk

Targeted web/index searches were made for thesis/dissertation, workshop and
conference records using the exact family, inverse-EGZ terminology and the
Girard--Schmid title/notation. The searches surfaced older general inverse
zero-sum theses/projects and adjacent current papers, but no record that could
be relied on as an exact CAND-02 completion.

Likewise, the bounded searches for m=2 and low equal factors did not locate a
published classification of C_2 + C_4 + C_4 extremals, nor a prime-only or
prime-power-only theorem that materially compresses the all-m contribution.

These are search non-hits only. Unindexed theses, talks, manuscripts,
unpublished results or equivalent formulations can still exist.
