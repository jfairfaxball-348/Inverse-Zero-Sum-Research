## Primary statements rechecked for proof verification

**Gao--Hui--Li--Li--Qu--Zhong, Acta Arith. 212 (2024), 133--172,
DOI 10.4064/aa230118-1-10.**

The author-hosted primary PDF was rechecked at Lemma 3.3. Item (2)'s proof takes
a short-free `T` of length `eta(G)-1`, uses Property C to write
`T=U^(n-1)`, and states that `S=U^n` has no innerly
non-zero-sum-joint short zero-sum subsequences. Item (3)'s proof decomposes an
avoiding sequence into short minimal zero sums and then, for every packed
block, chooses an element from its support; the deletion conclusions follow
from avoidance. The generic choice supplies the representative-swap
quantifier used by S040.

The same PDF was checked at Lemma 3.1: its appeal to the earlier GGW11/GPZ13
results is explicitly under the hypothesis `r in [1,2]` or `n=2`, not the
`C_3^3` target.

Official publication metadata: volume 212 (2024), pages 133--172, published
online 19 January 2024.
