## Proposal summary

The project concerns Gao's rank-two Property D conjecture for C_n^2: every sequence of length 4n-4 with no n-term zero-sum should have form T^(n-1). Gao proved multiplicativity, so prime moduli are the essential cases. Historical work covers small moduli, and Schlage-Puchta's 2025 preprint proves Property D for every sufficiently large prime but gives only an existential cutoff. A 2010 conference abstract by Schlage-Puchta, described as joint work with Gautami Bhowmik, had already announced the large-prime conclusion. The programme is therefore not assuming from search failure that the remaining all-prime problem is open. The proposed contribution is an all-prime completion or substantial structural advance eliminating the residual exceptional-prime layer, with E-JC as the intended venue if the result is substantial enough.
