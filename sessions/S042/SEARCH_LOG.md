## Serious source trails

1. Gao--Hui--Xue Li--Yuanlin Li--Qu--Zhong, *On generalized Narkiewicz
   constants of finite abelian groups*, Acta Arith. 212 (2024), 133--172,
   DOI 10.4064/aa230118-1-10. Defines `eta^N`, proves the direct
   `C_3^3` value and constructs the `U^3` avoiding family; no converse
   classification of every length-24 extremal. **PA-INGREDIENT.**

2. Gao--Geroldinger--Wang, *A quantitative aspect of non-unique
   factorizations: the Narkiewicz constants*, IJNT 7 (2011), 1463--1502,
   DOI 10.1142/S1793042111004721. Primary text p. 1472 gives Conjecture 3.4
   for `C_n\oplus C_n` and explicitly says the paper does not work on the
   inverse problem. **PA-INGREDIENT.**

3. Gao--Li--Peng, *Narkiewicz constants II*, Colloq. Math. 124 (2011),
   205--218, DOI 10.4064/cm124-2-5; Gao--Peng--Zhong,
   *Narkiewicz constants III*, Acta Arith. 158 (2013), 271--285,
   DOI 10.4064/aa158-3-6. Rank-two `N_1` antecedents.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**

4. Fan--Hui--Zhong, Colloq. Math. 176 (2024), 207--217,
   DOI 10.4064/cm9413-10-2024. Revised/Narkiewicz-sense inverse structure
   for rank-two `C_n\oplus C_{nm}` in the stated range.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**

5. Fan--Zhong, JCTA 212 (2025), 105984,
   DOI 10.1016/j.jcta.2024.105984. Joint short minimal zero-sum theory
   explicitly over rank-two groups. **PA-INGREDIENT.**

6. Hui--Zhong, JCTA 224 (2026), 106238,
   DOI 10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106238. Current primary record: completely
   settles the inverse problem for rank two; its literature positioning says
   larger rank has only a few results and points back to the 2024 direct line.
   Strong current-status control, but not a global novelty proof.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**

7. Fan--Gao--Wang--Zhong--Zhuang, E-JC 19(3) (2012), #P31,
   DOI 10.37236/2602. Full-rank ordinary short-zero-sum ingredients used by
   S040/S041, not the generalized-Narkiewicz inverse theorem.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**

8. Gao--Thangadurai, *On the structure of sequences with forbidden zero-sum
   subsequences*, Colloq. Math. 98 (2003), 213--222,
   DOI 10.4064/cm98-2-7. Ordinary short-zero-sum-free longest-sequence
   structure for `n=3^a,d=3`; different invariant/length.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**

9. Edel--Elsholtz--Geroldinger--Kubertin--Rackham, *Zero-sum problems in
   finite abelian groups and affine caps*, Q. J. Math. 58 (2007), 159--186,
   DOI 10.1093/qmath/ham003. Lemma 5.4 classifies maximal affine caps for
   `AG(r,3)`, including `r=3`. A squarefree short-free length-8 core
   `U` corresponds to the 9-cap `U\cup{0}`, but cap classification does
   not force multiplicity three for an arbitrary target multisequence.
   **PA-INGREDIENT.**
