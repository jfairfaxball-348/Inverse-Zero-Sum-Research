## Key source/status conclusions

Girard--Schmid's 2019 direct theorem gives
`s(C_2+C_{2m}+C_{2m})=8m+1`. Their 2020 inverse theorem is for the
non-isomorphic family `C_2+C_2+C_{2n}`; `m=1` is the already classified
`C_2^3` endpoint and is correctly excluded from CAND-02.

The audit found later work on the same rank-three group architecture for
different invariants, especially Li--Yin's `disc(G)` work, but no theorem
located in the bounded search that classifies the exact ordinary-EGZ
length-`8m` extremal sequences for all `m>=2`, no small-parameter theorem
for `m=2`, and no source explicitly declaring the exact problem open.
CAND-02 therefore survives as **SOURCE-DEFINED / CURRENT STATUS UNKNOWN**, not
as a certified-open problem.

Its substantial surviving scope is a full all-`m>=2` structural
classification, or a broad uniform theorem materially advancing that
classification. A routine isolated finite case is not the intended result
unless it supplies a reusable mechanism or closes a demonstrably complete
remaining layer.
