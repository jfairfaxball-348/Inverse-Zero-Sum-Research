## Initial, final, consent and concurrency rules
- Initial E-JC manuscript is a **PDF** including a short *informative, entirely self-contained abstract*; the web form also requests an HTML rendition of the abstract, with no user-defined LaTeX macros. Paper must be original, self-contained, grammatically unambiguous English, substantial, and correctly attribute methods; **no fixed page limit**.
- **Do not upload initial TeX source**; final accepted paper **only if accepted** uses the journal's supplied `e-jc.sty` (not modified), journal sample, PDF plus all source material, consolidated LaTeX/bibliography and archives of imported images as appropriate. No presumption of acceptance.
- All authors consent, no simultaneous consideration for publication elsewhere, no prior publication of the same work, and **no author must have submitted any other paper to E-JC within the last three calendar months**, including rejected submissions. This personal-history attestation is unverified and deferred to actual submission.
- E-JC holds an owner-granted worldwide irrevocable royalty-free right to publish/distribute/archive; authors retain copyright and the journal encourages Creative Commons. The existing GitHub repository Apache-2.0 license does **not** fix the paper's eventual arXiv license.
