## Faithful proposition and position semantics
`G=Fin(3) → ZMod(3)` in Lean (isomorphic to `C_3^3`), `Alphabet={g:G // g≠0}`, `PosSeq n=Fin n→Alphabet`, `ShortZero S I` means a nonempty `I⊆Fin n`, cardinality at most three, group sum zero. `AvoidsInnerJointPair S` quantifies over *both position sets*, and `innerJointSum S I J = posSum S (I∩J)`; absence of a forbidden pair is a zero intersection sum. `IsTriplePower S U` allows a permutation of all 24 positions matching `tripleRep U`. The latter is a commutative sequence equality, not a stronger fixed-order statement. These are S043 semantic correspondences, later actually proved through S057.

## Human-readable dependency graph (published input **P**, programme deduction **N**)
1. **P1, 2024/Gao et al.**: indexed `η^N` definition/Lemma 2.1; Property C, `s(G)=19`, `η(G)=17`; proof of Lemma 3.3(3) licenses maximal disjoint short minimal zero-sum packing and **every** representative transversal's survivor short-freeness, with first `l` deletion excluding 3-term zero sums.
2. **N1, S039:** write `S=B_1⋯B_s S_0`; `l` length-three short atoms, `s-l` length-two atoms and `r=|S_0|`. Then `24=l+2s+r`, `l≥6` from `24-l≤18=s(G)-1`, `s≥8` from `24-s≤16=η(G)-1`. Feasible triples are **exactly** `(6,8,2),(7,8,1),(8,8,0),(6,9,0)`. The transversal residual has length `24-s` and is short-free for every choice.
3. **P2, 2012/Fan et al.**: Lemma 28 gives `σ(R)=0` for every short-free 16-term `R`. Lemma 7/Property C/eta 17, and Lemma 29(1) for auxiliary length-15 nonzero total.
4. **N2, S040 s=8:** residual `R_x` length16; fix all selected representatives but one in `B_j`. For selectable values `a,b`, two residual sums `σ(S)-t-a=0=σ(S)-t-b` force `a=b`. Thus each packed block constant; a 2-term zero-sum in `C_3^3\{0}` is `a(-a)` and cannot be constant; so `(6,8,2),(7,8,1)` excluded. Eight 3-atoms, each `u_i^3`, give `S=U^3`; survivor `U^2` short-free, requiring the `u_i` distinct (three copies of a repeated value would sum zero). Thus `U` squarefree, |U|=8 and short-free.
5. **N3, S040 length15:** every short-free 15-term residual has each multiplicity ≤2; support ≥8. If ≥9, choose 9 *distinct* values as `T`; `T` is short-free and `T^2` also short-free because an exponent-three zero-sum triple with a repeated value must consist of three identical values; with `|T^2|=18` this violates `η(G)=17`. Thus support exactly8, profile `2^7 1`. Let `u` be its unique singleton. Adding a second `u` maintains short-freeness (proof requires analysing witnesses using both copies), giving short-free length16 `Ru`; by P2, `σ(R)+u=0`, so `u=-σ(R)`. Fan Lemma29 also says `σ(R)≠0`, consistent with u nonzero.
6. **N4, S040 s=9:** signature `(6,9,0)` has six 3-atoms, three necessarily nonconstant 2-atoms and no remainder; `σ(S)=0`. For any transversal with representative sum `d`, survivor R has unique singleton `u=-σ(R)=d`. Fix a nonconstant 2-atom and all other representatives with complementary sum `t`. Change chosen `a` to `b≠a` and compare residual fibre counts: only `(0,1)→(1,0)` or `(1,2)→(2,1)` retain `2^7 1`. In case 1 singleton identities `b=t+a, a=t+b` imply `2(a-b)=0`, hence a=b in exponent 3, impossible. Case 2 identities force `t=0`. Because the choice in the *second* nonconstant 2-atom among the complement can be changed, `t` cannot be zero for **every** transversal. Contradiction; `s=9` impossible.
7. **N5, classification necessity:** only `(8,8,0)` survives; real position permutation identifies S with exactly three occurrences of each term of the squarefree short-free core U. S056 proves faithful `Fin 24` reindexing, not only support equality.
8. **P3 direct construction and N6 converse:** Gao et al. 2024 Lemma3.3(2) shows `U^3` avoiding under Property C. S057 instead gives a direct formal proof: any short zero-sum in `tripleRep U` must be a full 3-position constant fibre (two-term opposite pair excluded by U shortfree; distinct triple also excluded); two such witnesses have empty or entire-fibre intersection; their intersection sum is zero. Reindex to arbitrary positional S through inverse permutation. This step is elementary but must still be checked by author.
