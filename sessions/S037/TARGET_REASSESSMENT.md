## 6. Bounded discovery — new CAND-06

### Exact target

Let `G=C_5^3`. Classify all zero-sum sequences
`U in B(G)` with `|U|=D_4(G)=30` that cannot be partitioned into five
nontrivial zero-sum subsequences. This is exactly the standard inverse problem
associated to the fourth generalized Davenport constant.

### Source basis

Sze Chun Yiu's September 2026 preprint proves `D_4(C_5^3)=30` and hence
`D_k(C_5^3)=5k+10` for all `k>=2`. The proof is finite and
computer-assisted, with saturation reducing multiplicities to `{1,2,4}`,
a finite support-pattern system, and a finite rank/plane branch cover; the
paper reports three independent implementations and ancillary verification.

Qinghai Zhong's 2025 Combinatorica paper gives the exact standard inverse
`D_k` formulation and completely solves it for rank-two groups. Thus CAND-06
has a source-defined inverse genre and a fresh exact direct threshold without
a hidden direct-constant conjecture.

A focused search through 2026-10-06 found no checked source classifying the
inverse `D_4(C_5^3)` extremals. Because the direct result is only about one
month old and currently a preprint, collision/current-status risk is
substantially higher than for CAND-03. The search non-hit is not evidence of
openness.

### Assessment

- Exact baseline / hypotheses: **high as a source statement, provisional as a
  fresh preprint result**.
- Current-status confidence: **low-medium**.
- Novelty/collision risk: **high** because the direct result is extremely fresh.
- Parameter breadth: **fixed group, fixed k**.
- Entry machinery: Yiu's saturation/support/plane geometry plus the mature
  rank-two inverse `D_k` framework.
- First runnable dependency after owner selection: reproduce/verify the direct
  baseline and ancillary branch cover, then test whether inverse extremals
  inherit a theorem-level saturation/support constraint before any catalogue.
- Meaningful partial-result space: potentially strong if it yields a structural
  inverse theorem or reusable rank-three `D_k` lemma; a brute-force list is
  below the programme bar.
- Publication fit: plausible E-JC/JCTA/Combinatorica-level subject fit if a
  genuinely structural result is obtained.
- Reviewer route: Grynkiewicz or Yuan are plausible independent status leads;
  Yiu and directly adjacent source authors are not automatically independent
  reviewers.
- Early falsification: stop if a current-status check finds parallel/known
  inverse work, if direct verification fails, or if the inverse problem
  immediately collapses to a large irreducible computational catalogue.

**Disposition: viable but provisional new alternative; below CAND-03.**
