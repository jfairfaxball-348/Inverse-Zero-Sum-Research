## CAND-06 — inverse fourth generalized Davenport constant on C_5^3

### ZS-68 — Yiu 2026 direct theorem

Sze Chun Yiu, *The fourth generalized Davenport constant of C_5^3*,
arXiv:2609.04950v1, submitted 2026-09-04:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04950

The source proves `D_4(C_5^3)=30` and hence
`D_k(C_5^3)=5k+10` for every `k>=2`. The proof is computer-assisted
and reports a saturation reduction, 60 support-pattern solutions, 78
rank/plane branches, three independent implementations, and ancillary
verification. Because this result is extremely fresh and currently a preprint,
S037 treats its direct baseline as source-defined but requiring a pre-proof
verification checkpoint if selected.

### ZS-69 — Zhong 2025 inverse D_k framework

Qinghai Zhong, *On the Inverse Problem of the k-th Davenport Constants for
Groups of Rank 2*, Combinatorica 45 (2025), article 31.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00153-3

The paper defines
`M_k(G)={S in B(G): S cannot be partitioned into k+1 nontrivial zero sums}`
and `D_k(G)=max{|S|:S in M_k(G)}`, and completely solves the associated
inverse problem for rank-two groups. This makes the proposed CAND-06 statement
a standard source-defined inverse problem, not a newly invented invariant.

Focused searches combining `C_5^3`, `D_4`, generalized Davenport and
inverse/partition terminology located the Yiu direct preprint and the rank-two
inverse line but no checked source classifying the length-30 inverse
`C_5^3` extremals. Given the one-month age of the direct result, this
non-hit carries especially little openness weight.
