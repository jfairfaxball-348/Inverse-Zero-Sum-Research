## Purpose

S020 is the required periodic audit for S011–S020. It is an audit and strategy
reassessment, not a fresh proof session. Its purpose is to test whether the
programme is still advancing the root objective of a substantial, publishable
mathematical contribution rather than accumulating equivalent reformulations.

authoritative/START_HERE.md, all required registers/protocol, the complete

1. Inventory S011–S019 durable mathematical and source outputs, distinguishing

2. Recheck corrections, proof boundaries, computation reproducibility and all
   route-stop decisions.

   extension saturation -> replacement-core incidence -> near-full threshold
   stars -> 2-primary counting -> higher-length incidence -> binary design
   realization.

   constraints through codegree n admit every S016 delta=0/1/2 signature.
   This is not an actual zero-sum-layer realization and does not resolve the
   remaining modulo-4 split.
5. Reassess target status, novelty risk, publication credibility and external
   review without inferring openness or endorsement from search failure or
   reviewer silence.
6. Identify the smallest live mathematical dependencies that genuinely bear on
   full CAND-02 after all stopped routes are removed.

## Anti-churn and source boundaries

D-050 and D-057 remain binding. Do not reopen D7–D10 local-hole/capacity/Fano,
the progressive-block route, S016 shifted residual/cross-boundary exchanges, or
another equivalent binary-incidence layer split. Do not perform a fresh proof
attempt simply because the audit reaches a possible idea.

Mixed-primary continuation requires an exact primary-source theorem preserving
the full-group prescribed length/sum. A bounded source non-hit is not evidence
that the target or a subproblem is open or novel.

## Required disposition

The audit must end in exactly one of two states:

- **READY S021:** one genuinely distinct, source- and hypothesis-justified
  bounded dependency is identified. Prepare its brief and one live prompt, but
  do not execute it inside S020; or
- **OWNER BLOCKER:** no route clears the anti-churn bar. Activate a concrete
  strategy/target blocker, suppress the live prompt, and request only the owner
  decision required by protocol.

S020 must synchronize authority, run the repository checker and warranted
checks, publish safely to main, and verify the outgoing SHA/tree.
