## Two-stage request

1. **Proposal assessment:** ask whether the exact question, known baseline,
   intended contribution and proposed journals make mathematical sense. Do not
   ask someone to promise that we will solve it or have it accepted.
2. **Manuscript review:** ask whether they would be willing in principle to
   independently examine a later completed paper, conditional on scope, timing
   and availability. Record whether this is unpaid, paid, or otherwise arranged
   if relevant, without assuming a commercial service is journal peer review.

Record agreement date, the actual permitted scope, evidence location (a minimal
owner-confirmed summary is acceptable), conditions and any later withdrawal.
An initial reply, arXiv endorsement or agreement to receive a PDF is insufficient.
If different people cover the two stages, document both rather than assuming one
person covers everything. Reconfirm availability when the manuscript is ready.

## Role boundary

Our reviewer provides pre-submission scrutiny. The journal independently chooses
referees and applies conflicts rules. Neither review agreement nor a positive
private report is journal acceptance. Preserve critical feedback as well as
positive feedback. A reviewer developing new proofs may require a different
contribution attribution; do not force substantive collaboration into a reviewer
label solely to keep single authorship.

When a reviewer approach becomes the next required action, prepare the proposal
and draft first, activate an owner blocker, request sending/authorization, and
close with **no next-session prompt**. Waiting for a reply cannot be bypassed
with an assumption or another research kickoff.
