## S001 target-selection resolution

B-001 is **RESOLVED** by D-010. The selected target is CAND-01:

> For every integer `n >= 2`, does `C_n^2` have Property D; equivalently,
> must every sequence of length `4n-4` over `C_n^2` with no zero-sum
> subsequence of length `n` have Property-D form `T^(n-1)`?

The owner selected the mathematical identity, not a claim that the conjecture is
currently unresolved in every modulus and not a prediction that it is provable
or publishable. S002 must perform a target-specific current-status and overlap
audit before the target gate can open.
