## Claim classifications

EXPERIMENTAL, FORMALISED, PRIVATE_REVIEW, JOURNAL_ACCEPTED, JOURNAL_PUBLISHED.
Record exact scope, hypotheses, provenance, dependency gaps and verification
boundary. A claim can have several classifications only when each is supported.
Do not turn an abstract, author assertion or LLM review into a checked proof.

## Source discipline for later sessions

For every substantive literature decision, record author/title, publication or
preprint status, DOI/URL, version and retrieval date, exact theorem/conjecture
identifier, access level, relevant quantifiers and how it relates to our claim.
Prefer original papers; surveys guide discovery. Third-party indexes are leads,
not proof-status authority. A full-text access failure is an explicit gap.
Re-audit the actual proved statement before novelty claims or submission.

Do not mirror full copyrighted papers into the repo without permission. Keep
bounded quotations or original summaries and link to primary sources. Research
source retrieval does not authorize contacting authors.
